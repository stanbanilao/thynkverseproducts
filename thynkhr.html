What it does

One clearer home for everyday people operations.

Thynk HR is designed to reduce scattered employee administration while keeping access, status and responsibility visible to the right roles.

01 / PEOPLE

Employee administration

Keep core employee information and operational status in one structured place instead of across disconnected files.

02 / ATTENDANCE

Attendance visibility

Give employees and authorised managers a clearer view of attendance and everyday workforce activity.

03 / TRAINING

Training progress

Track assigned training, SOP acknowledgement and completion status without relying on manual follow-up alone.

04 / ROLES

Role-based experience

Present the actions and information appropriate to employees, team leaders and HR administrators.
