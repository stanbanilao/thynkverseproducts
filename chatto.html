Chatto

Turn structured data into controlled conversations.

Chatto is a licensed Windows communication automation product for teams that work from Excel or CSV lists and need consistent, personalised outbound messaging without turning the process into a manual copy-and-paste exercise.

What it does

Map data once, apply the right message template, queue or schedule the send, and keep results visible for review.

Each function below describes the customer-facing operational capability without exposing proprietary implementation detail or internal automation logic.

01 / MAP

Flexible spreadsheet mapping

Handle Excel or CSV input, including multi-sheet files, by mapping the fields needed for the communication workflow.

02 / TEMPLATE

Status-driven messages

Use approved templates and dynamic variables so the message changes with the record status instead of relying on manual rewriting.

03 / QUEUE

Clean and controlled sending

Normalise phone numbers, hold unknown statuses, and queue or schedule approved messages before they are sent.

04 / TRACE

Visible outcomes

Keep results and activity visible so the team can see what was sent, what was held and what needs attention.

How the work moves

A controlled workflow from input to verified outcome.

The product is designed around a specific operational job, with automation where it is repeatable and human attention where judgment is needed.
