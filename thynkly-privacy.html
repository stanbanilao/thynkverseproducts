Thynkly Lead Gen

Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026. This policy covers the Thynkly Lead Gen browser extension for Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge, published by Thynkverse (FM Consulting ZA), Cape Town, South Africa.

What Thynkly is

Thynkly Lead Gen is a business tool. It works only for companies that hold an active Thynkly licence and only after a company Supervisor issues a link code. It counts dialer calls and saved call results, and captures a submitted iDocs Quick Capture, and sends these to the company's own Thynkverse CRM.

What the extension collects

  • South African ID number of an iDocs Quick Capture, only when the form is submitted.
  • Dialer sign-in details from the Dialog Connect page: the dialer username, sign-in email address, role and expiry. Nothing else from that record is kept.
  • Call activity: the number of calls and saved call results (dispositions) for the person signed into the dialer, per day.
  • Link code and CRM address, entered by you once to link the PC.

The extension does not collect client names, phone numbers, addresses, financial details, passwords, browsing history, location, or the content of messages. It runs only on i-docs.co.za, Dialog Connect (dialogconnect.com) and the Thynkverse CRM.

Why, and where it goes

The data is used for one purpose: to credit calls and captures to the correct Lead Gen or Sales Agent in the company's Thynkverse CRM. It is sent over HTTPS to the company's own CRM address (crm.thynkverse.co.za or a company subdomain of it) and to nowhere else. Thynkly has no advertising, analytics or tracking, and the data is not sold or shared with third parties. It is not used to assess creditworthiness or for lending.

The link code is checked by Thynkverse Super Control to confirm the company's licence and seat. If the person signed into the dialer is not set up in the CRM or has no active seat, nothing is counted.

Storage and retention

The extension keeps its working data (link code, CRM address, daily counts and a short queue of items waiting to send) in your browser's local extension storage. Removing the extension deletes it. Data held in the company's CRM is kept under that company's own CRM retention arrangements, as the company is the responsible party for its staff and client information.

Your rights (POPIA)

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act you may ask what is held about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Ask your company Supervisor first, as the company controls its CRM data. You can also write to Thynkverse at accounts@thynkverse.co.za.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Questions: accounts@thynkverse.co.za.
