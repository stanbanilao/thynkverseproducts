What it does

Less repetitive capture. More control at the final decision.

Designed to support authorised debt review operations without replacing the judgement or registration responsibilities of the debt counsellor.

01 / RECEIVE

CRM-directed work

Processes approved Flagging jobs only for the licensed company, environment and authorised worker target.

02 / CHECK

Evidence from iDocs

Checks the linked applicant profile, ID copy and Form 16 before preparing the NCR form.

03 / PREPARE

NCR application preparation

Transfers supported client details into NCR and flags fields requiring attention instead of silently guessing.

04 / REVIEW

Save stays with the counsellor

Stops before NCR Save. The debt counsellor checks the form and decides whether to submit.

Controlled workflow

From one approved job to a verified human checkpoint.
